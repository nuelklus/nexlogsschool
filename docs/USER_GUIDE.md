# NLSchool user guide

This guide describes the application in the current project version. Menu items
and actions depend on your role. Your school administrator should provide the
application address and help you get an account.

> **Testing notice:** The project is not yet approved for production use.
> Academic and finance data-integrity safeguards and deployment checks remain
> outstanding. Use an isolated test environment with fictional students and
> simulated payments until the school and operator have completed a separate
> readiness review. Do not treat test invoices or receipts as real accounting
> records.

## 1. Sign in and navigate

1. Open the address provided by your school or system administrator.
2. Sign in with your email address and password. If you received an
   invitation, open the invitation link and follow its instructions first.
3. If asked, verify your email. Use the password-reset option on the sign-in
   screen if you have forgotten your password.
4. If you belong to more than one school, check the **School** selector before
   viewing or entering information.
5. Use the menu on the left to open the available sections. Sign out when
   finished, particularly on a shared device.

### Roles at a glance

| Role | Typical tasks |
| --- | --- |
| Owner / school administrator | Configure the school, admit students, manage classes, create examinations, publish results and report cards, promote students and manage finance. |
| Teacher | Work with assigned classes and subjects, record attendance, and enter and submit marks for assigned examinations. |
| Headteacher | Review submitted examinations, approve or return results, and review report cards. |
| Accountant | Use finance features according to the assigned campus permissions. |
| Parent / guardian | View linked children's information and published academic results and reports available in their portal. |
| Student | View their own profile and published academic results and reports available in their portal. |

These are a summary, not a guarantee of access to every button. Some actions
have additional permissions or state requirements. If an option is missing,
ask your school administrator to check your role and assignments.

## 2. Set up a school

The owner or an administrator should complete the following before admission
or billing:

1. In **Overview**, create an academic year with its start and end dates.
   Mark the appropriate year as current.
2. Create the school's terms within that year. Terms are entered manually;
   they are not generated automatically.
3. In **Overview → Campuses**, create at least one active campus, even for
   a single-campus school. For example, create **Main Campus**.
4. Under **Overview → Class levels & classes**, set up the school's class
   levels and create class groups for the academic year. Select the correct
   active campus and class level for each class. A section and room can be
   entered where appropriate.
5. In **Academic Management → Subjects & teachers**, add subjects and assign
   teachers to the relevant class and subject.
6. Invite staff through **Staff**, choosing the appropriate available role.
   Each person should use their own account.

Class name and section must currently be unique within an academic year
across the whole school, including across campuses. Existing historical
classes are not automatically assigned a campus or class level.

**Example setup:** Create year **2026/2027**, its three terms, **Main
Campus**, class level **Basic 1**, class group **Basic 1 A**, and subjects
such as Mathematics and Agriculture. Assign a teacher to each class-subject
combination. Use dates that match the school's actual academic calendar.

## 3. Manage students and invitations

### Admit a student

1. Open **Students** in the left menu.
2. Under **Enrol a student**, enter the student's name and requested details.
3. Select the academic year, campus if applicable, class group and enrollment
   date. The date must be within the selected academic year.
4. Select **Enrol student**. The admission number is generated by the system.
   Admission creates both the student record and initial class enrollment.
5. Check the success message and the new record in the roster. A student does
   not need a portal account to be enrolled.

### Find a student

On the **Students** roster:

- Search by name, admission number or grade.
- Combine filters for class group, campus, status, gender and portal access.
- Use **Clear filters** to reset the search and filters.
- Select the student's name to open a read-only details dialog. It includes
  their current-year class, campus, profile, account summary and most
  recently created non-void invoice. That invoice is not necessarily for the
  current term. Select **Close** or press Escape to return to the roster.

The class filter uses a unique active enrollment in the current academic year.
**No unique current class** includes students with no active enrollment for
that year and those with more than one; their records need checking. Campus
may fall back to the student profile if enrollment evidence is unavailable.

### Invite a guardian or student

1. Find the student and expand **Invite family or student**.
2. Enter the guardian's email and relationship; mark the primary guardian if
   appropriate. Send the invitation.
3. To give the student their own login, use **Invite student portal** and
   enter the student's email address.
4. The recipient follows the invitation email. If an invitation fails or
   expires, contact the school administrator rather than sharing passwords.

## 4. Manage classes, attendance and timetables

In **Academic Management**, owners and administrators can manage classes,
subjects and teacher assignments. Class setup is also available in
**Overview → Class levels & classes**.

### Class enrollment and subjects

Admission creates the student's initial class enrollment. In **Academic
Management → Enrollment**, find a student by name or admission number and
select a class to see its subjects and any existing enrollment. Create an
enrollment only when the student does not already have one for that class.
Every enrolled student follows all subjects assigned to the class under
**Subjects & teachers**. Changes to class assignments apply automatically;
individual subject restrictions are no longer used.

### Attendance

1. Open **Academic Management → Attendance**.
2. Under **Class attendance register**, select the academic year, class and
   date.
3. For each unrecorded student, choose the appropriate attendance status and
   add notes if needed.
4. Save the register and check the confirmation. Existing records are shown
   but are not changed by this register submission.

Teachers can work with assigned classes; administrators can manage the
register according to their permissions. Attendance filters allow staff to
review recorded entries.

### Timetable and class changes

Use **Academic Management → Timetable** to add lessons with a class, subject,
teacher, weekday and time. Set up the class and teacher assignments first.

To move a student between classes **within the current academic year**, use
**Academic Management → Enrollment → Transfer student to another class**.
Select the current enrollment, destination class and effective date, and
enter the reason. The destination class's subjects apply automatically. Existing attendance
and results remain with the original enrollment; existing invoices do not
change. Cross-campus moves are not supported, and transferred-student report
creation during a term currently requires reconciliation.

## 5. Exams and results

### Prepare and create examinations

1. Open **Academic Management → Exams & results**.
2. An owner or administrator creates a grading configuration with the score
   components, weights and grade bands. Review **Saved grading
   configurations** before creating exams. Editing the latest configuration
   creates a new version; existing exams keep their original version.
3. Create an examination for **one class and one subject**, not one per
   student. Select the term, class, subject, configuration, examination dates,
   marks deadline and publication date.
4. To create several new class-subject examinations together, use **Create
   examination session**. Each entry has its own schedule and workflow.
   Sessions create new examinations; they do not attach existing ones. If an
   Agriculture exam already exists, leave it out unless another Agriculture
   exam is intended.
5. Use **Created examinations** to search with term, class and status
   filters. Select **Open** to go to the exam details. Use **Refresh
   examinations** or **Load session progress** when needed.

### Enter and publish marks

1. The assigned teacher opens the exam, enters each student's component
   status and score, and selects **Save draft marks**.
2. The teacher checks the saved marks and selects **Submit saved marks**
   after the examination ends. Save unsaved changes before opening another
   exam.
3. A headteacher reviews the submission and either approves it or returns it
   with a reason.
4. An owner or administrator publishes approved results on or after the
   publication date. Parents and students can then see their own published
   results.

Missing or absent marks can block finalization; zero is a score, not an
absence. The marks deadline is informational, not an automatic lock.
Corrections to published results require a new version and another
submission, review and publication; the previous publication remains visible
until the replacement is published.

## 6. Report cards

Publishing examination results and publishing report cards are **separate
steps**.

1. Publish one appropriate examination result for every subject assigned to
   the student's class.
2. Open an exam for the relevant class and term, and find **Official report
   cards**.
3. The headteacher chooses **Review a report card** for one student, or
   **Review class report cards** for a dated class roster. Select the
   published subject results and enter the requested class teacher and
   headteacher remarks.
4. For class review, check each student's selections and remarks before
   submitting. If any student fails validation, no reports in that batch are
   saved.
5. An owner or administrator selects **Load report cards**, inspects the
   reviewed reports and publishes each one. The list includes accessible
   reports across the school, not only the open class.
6. To download a class ZIP, select **Download class report cards**, choose
   the roster date and download. A published report must exist for every
   enrollment on that dated roster. The ZIP does not create or publish
   reports.

If a reviewed version is already awaiting publication, load and publish that
version rather than reviewing it again. Report-card creation for transfers
during the term is not yet supported; do not assume the system will combine
the student's records automatically.

## 7. Promote students

Promotion places students into a **later academic year**. For a class change
within the current year, use the transfer workflow instead.

1. Create the next academic year. Its start date must be **after** the source
   year's end date.
2. Create the destination class groups for that year and assign their
   campuses. Current promotion requires consistent same-campus evidence.
3. Open **Academic Management → Promotion**.
4. Choose **Promote from**, **Promote into** and the **Source class**; select
   **Load students**.
5. Review each row. Choose **Promote** and a destination class, **Repeat
   class**, **Graduate**, or **Leave as is**. Suggestions may already be
   selected: check every row. Use **— Choose —** to leave a student out of
   the operation.
6. Check the number shown on **Apply to … students** and apply. Repeat for
   the next class. Bulk actions affect only the loaded class.

**Make the new year the current academic year** is unchecked by default
because it changes the year for the whole school. If the destination year is
already current, applying placements updates placed students' profile class
used by parts of billing even if that box is unchecked. Promotion creates
the new enrollment and retains previous enrollment history; it does not
create invoices. Students follow the subjects assigned to their destination
class.

## 8. Fees, invoices and payments

### Set up fees

1. Open **Finance → Fees**. Review the fee categories and add any that are
   missing, choosing an income account for each category. An owner or
   administrator can add an income account on this page if needed.
2. Under **Set a fee**, select the academic year, fee category and class
   level or all class levels. Enter the amount, applicability and effective
   dates.
3. For optional or individual services, set up the student's subscription
   under **Finance → Services**. Per-unit fees also need the relevant usage.
4. Review **Fee schedules** before generating invoices. Search by category
   or class level, or filter by academic year and status. Check the effective
   dates and activate or deactivate schedules as needed. Class-level fees
   rely on the class's configured level. Saving a schedule does not create
   an invoice, and changes do not alter previously issued invoices.

### Preview, create and issue invoices

1. Open **Finance → Billing**.
2. To bill one student, choose **Bill a student**, select the student, year,
   term and optional due date, then create the draft.
3. For a class, use **Preview and bill a class**. Select the year, term and
   actual class or section for that year, run the preview, and check each
   student's fees, excluded items, warnings and any blockers. The class's
   linked level determines which class-level fees apply. Only students with
   an eligible enrollment in the selected class are included; enroll students
   without a class enrollment or bill them individually.
4. Create drafts for eligible students, review them and issue them when
   correct. Issuing makes invoices visible to eligible parents and posts
   them to the ledger. Check any per-student failures.
5. Use **Finance → Invoices** to open and review invoices.

For third-term fees, select the old year and term. For the new year's first
term, create the new classes and fees, promote students, then preview and
issue the new-year invoices. Billing normally resolves the class enrollment
at the start of the selected term. For a student who joins later in the term,
it uses the first class enrollment during that term and charges the full
configured fee, without automatic proration. The preview shows a warning for
late enrollment. If no suitable enrollment exists, it may use the profile
class and show a warning. Always inspect the preview.
Promotion does not change existing invoices.

An issued invoice's detail may show **Brought forward from earlier
invoices** and **Total amount due**. This is a display of earlier unpaid
balances, not a second charge; the original invoices remain payable. The
amount is live and can change after payments or credits. Currently, “earlier”
means created earlier, not necessarily an earlier term or issue date. An
accountant's view may be limited to authorized campuses. Draft invoices do
not show brought-forward balances as issued receivables.

### Record a payment

1. Open **Finance → Payments** and search for the student.
2. Select the payment method and enter the amount, date and required
   reference. Enter the payer where appropriate.
3. If **Apply to the oldest outstanding invoices first** is selected, the
   system allocates to outstanding invoices in due-date order; extra money is
   held as an advance.
4. Record the payment and check its status. Mobile-money entries stay
   **pending** until staff confirm that the money arrived. A confirmed
   payment has a receipt available from the payment history.

Online payment gateways are not configured. Do not mark a mobile-money
payment confirmed until it has actually been verified outside the system.
Use authorized correction workflows instead of entering a duplicate payment.

## 9. Other finance tools

The Finance menu also includes **Arrears**, **Advances**, **Reports**,
**Transactions**, **Other income**, **Money received**, **Accounting**,
**Suppliers**, **Supplier bills**, **Expenses** and **Payables**. Use these
according to the school's procedures and your permissions. Reports and
account balances should be reconciled with source records before being used
for a decision.

Reminder candidates can be reviewed in Finance, but automated SMS or
WhatsApp delivery is not configured. Accounting-period, approval, reversal
and campus restrictions may prevent actions; ask an owner or administrator
if you need help. Do not assume a report or invoice has updated older
financial records automatically.

## 10. Parents and students

1. Accept the invitation sent by the school and sign in using your own
   account.
2. Parents should check **Your children**; students should check **Your
   student profile**.
3. Look for published results and report cards in the school portal. Draft,
   submitted and reviewed records are not the same as published records.
4. If a child is missing, ask the school to send or check the guardian
   invitation. If a result or report is missing, ask the school to verify
   its publication status.

## 11. Common questions and errors

| Message or situation | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| “Choose a later academic year that starts after the source year ends.” | Check both years' dates. The destination must start after the source year ends; use transfer instead for a same-year class change. |
| “Select one published examination for every subject assigned to the class.” | Check the class's subject assignments. Create, submit, approve and publish the missing subject exams before reviewing the report. |
| “No reports were saved. Resolve each student error and retry.” | Class review is all-or-nothing. Read every student's error, correct the selections or records, and retry. |
| “A reviewed version already awaits publication.” | An owner or administrator should load and publish that reviewed report, or the headteacher should follow the available withdrawal process if it must be changed. |
| “Published reports are missing for…” during ZIP download | Publish a report for every enrollment on the selected roster date, then try the ZIP again. Published exam results alone are not enough. |
| A student is missing from a class filter | Check the current academic year and the student's active enrollment. **No unique current class** can indicate missing or conflicting enrollments. |
| A fee or class in the invoice preview is unexpected | Verify the selected year and term, term-start enrollment, class level, campus, fee schedule and preview warnings before creating or issuing invoices. |
| An action or page is unavailable | Check your role, class/subject assignment, school selection and subscription status. |

Do not repeatedly submit a form if an operation succeeded but its refresh
failed. Reload the relevant list and verify the record first to avoid
unintended duplicates.

## 12. Suggested test walkthrough

Use fictional names and simulated payments in a separate test environment:

1. Create one campus, an academic year, three terms, two class levels and
   their class groups. Add subjects and assign teachers.
2. Enrol two fictional students and confirm they appear in the correct class.
3. Configure fees, preview and issue a test invoice; record a simulated
   partial payment and inspect the balance and receipt.
4. Create examinations for the subjects, enter marks, submit, approve and
   publish results using the appropriate roles.
5. Review and publish both report cards; download the class ZIP.
6. Create the next academic year and classes, promote one class, then preview
   new-year billing. Verify old invoices and class history are unchanged.
7. Record any unexpected behavior and screenshots without including real
   personal or payment information.

Testing this workflow does not itself establish production readiness. An
operator must separately verify deployment, backups, access controls,
financial reconciliation and the unresolved data-integrity safeguards.
